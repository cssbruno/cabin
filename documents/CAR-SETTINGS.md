# Car Settings

Open Settings → Car Settings. The tab uses the same FYT feature gate as Teyes/FYT settings.

Expandable groups collect Lights, Mirrors and wipers, Parking & camera, Comfort, Audio & steering wheel, and My car. Verified hybrid profiles also show charging settings. My car contains appearance, trips, maintenance, compatibility and diagnostic export. Steering learning uses the same shared panel as Teyes/FYT settings; existing entry points remain available.

Factory choices use existing verified profile mappings and controller callbacks. Unknown profiles show “No verified mapping”. Missing feedback, disconnection, motion or missing callbacks disable factory writes. The existing parked-action guard runs before every factory selection; deferred selections are rejected after a profile change. Displayed choices change only on confirmed vehicle feedback. Climate opens the existing climate panel.

No new CAN mappings were introduced. Camera settings select the lens view; they do not add a camera video feed. Physical head-unit validation remains necessary.

Labels are localized in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
